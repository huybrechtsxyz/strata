| D1  | `deploy_log_path` has zero remote-backend support, while locks (ADR-0007) reuse the Terraform backend config and work items (ADR-0057) have pluggable S3/azblob/gcs backends. But two things soften this: (1) the *same* audit event that goes into the local deploy-log JSON is **already forwarded remotely** via SIEM sinks/webhook (`forward_to_siem()`, confirmed real and tested — see C3) — audit data isn't actually trapped on one machine, it just isn't retrievable *as a file* remotely.
