## Outcome

- **Verdict:** Keep "promotion" — it is the correct industry-standard term (Argo, Spinnaker, Octopus, GitLab all "promote") and reads cleanly as a command (`strata promote start`, `strata promote status`). No noun re-litigation.
- **Fix:** Drop `unpromote` as a CLI verb. Standardize the reverse direction on `strata promote rollback` (matches existing deploy-surface vocabulary). "Unpromotion" allowed only as ADR prose, never as a command.
- **Rule established:** Reverse operations follow user-facing vocabulary, not linguistic symmetry with the forward verb.
- **Action for ADR owner:** No structural change — ensure every CLI example uses `promote` (forward) and `promote rollback` (reverse); keep "unpromotion" descriptive only.
