## Outcome

- **Confirmed:** A lifecycle script hooked at `deploy_run_before` (before any stage touches infra) can act as a DIY precondition check today — fully achievable with existing mechanics.
- **`strata deploy status` — unreliable for this purpose:** deprecated/effectively just live terraform outputs, not a durable success record.
- **`strata deploy health` — footgun:** silently passes (`no_checks_defined`) when no `health_checks` are configured, so an unconfigured downstream layer would falsely appear healthy.
- **`strata deploy history` — recommended DIY signal:** per-execution `success` boolean is the more reliable disk-checkable signal of the three.
- **Caveat:** CI must persist/share `.strata/logs/` across ephemeral checkouts between the two layers' pipeline jobs for the history-based check to see prior executions.
